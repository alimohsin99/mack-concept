Company President

Patrick “Pat” Mackaronis

Pat Mackaronis formed Mack Construction in 2012 as a resident of Brooklyn, NY. The company started with smaller referred projects in Brooklyn and Queens, from warehouse renovations to private cement and concrete work.

As it grew, so did the team: heavy equipment and civil contracts, including removing copper telephone cables from Manhattan sewers and renovating public parks and underground utilities. Today the company operates from a full-service yard and warehouse in Greenpoint, Brooklyn, employing mostly New Yorkers from Brooklyn and Queens.
